Safety first: suspected pipeline leak or line break

  1. Stop. Do not approach if you smell hydrocarbons, see a vapour cloud, or hear gas escaping. Treat the area as hazardous (H2S possible).
  2. Upwind and uphill. Keep vehicles off the ROW and switch off ignition sources and phones near the site.
  3. Alert the control room by radio/satellite phone. Give pipeline name, nearest valve station, and what you observe.
  4. Confirm LBV/ESDV closure from the RTU panel local indicators only if it is safe to do so. Never bypass an ESD to restore flow.
  5. Follow the site Emergency Response Plan and your permit-to-work. Await instructions from the incident controller.

Remote RTU station checks

  • Battery voltage above low-voltage alarm; solar panel clean and unshaded.
  • Enclosure sealed, no moisture, no insect or dust ingress; SPD status indicators healthy.
  • Comms: note VSAT/cellular signal margin and last successful poll time.
  • Log all findings on paper and sync when the connection returns.
