Development & Architecture

UAE/GCC trunkline SCADA from wellhead to terminal: topologies, redundancy, telemetry links and remote solar valve stations.

Typical UAE / GCC pipeline SCADA topology

  • Control Centre (Dubai / Abu Dhabi): redundant SCADA servers, historian, DMZ with OPC UA gateway, ICS firewall zone separation per IEC 62443.
  • Backup control centre: hot-standby SCADA at a geographically separate site, with automatic failover and a tested manual changeover.
  • Station level: pump/compressor stations with redundant PLCs, local HMI and fire & gas, tied to the main comms backbone (fibre along ROW, microwave backup).
  • Remote Block Valve Stations (BVS/LBV): solar-powered RTU or compact PLC, dual comms path (cellular + VSAT or UHF), local ESD logic that must work with no comms.
  • Offshore: platform PLC/RTU, subsea umbilical or radio link, satellite fallback, tight weight and power budgets.
Rule of thumb: the safety action (valve closure on line break) is decided locally in the RTU/PLC. SCADA supervises and logs; it must never be the only path that closes a block valve.

Redundant hot-standby controller architecture

  • Siemens: S7-1500H or legacy S7-400H with synchronisation fibre; ET 200SP HA remote I/O with redundant PROFINET (S2 / R1 system redundancy).
  • Rockwell: ControlLogix redundancy (1756-RM2 modules) for availability. For safety functions use GuardLogix (SIL 3 capable) or a dedicated SIS; availability and safety integrity are separate design questions.
  • Define switchover time, diagnostics coverage, and what the I/O does during a changeover (hold last value vs. de-energise) in the Cause & Effect.

Remote solar-powered valve station: design notes

  • Power budget: size for the worst-case month plus 3 to 5 days of autonomy (dust on panels lowers yield 15 to 25 % between cleanings in desert conditions). Account for cellular/VSAT transmit peaks.
  • Batteries: temperature is the killer. Enclosure interior can exceed 60 °C in summer. Use sunshades, white or reflective coatings, and temperature-compensated charging (LiFePO4 with BMS, or VRLA derated for temperature).
  • Enclosure: 316L stainless NEMA 4X / IP66, sun shield gap, desiccant, breather drain. Mind ingress of fine dust at cable glands.
  • Protection: SPDs on solar input, 24 VDC loops, antenna coax and signal cables; bond to a low-resistance earth grid (dry sand has high resistivity; use chemical earthing).
  • Low-power modes: wake RTU on a schedule, report by exception, keep ESD logic and the valve controller always alive.

Engineering Design: HMI, C&E, ESD and Leak Detection

ISA-101 High-Performance HMI philosophy: a grayscale base where colour is reserved for abnormal conditions.

ISA-101 principles in one minute

  • Normal operation is neutral gray. If everything is colourful, nothing stands out when it matters.
  • Colour means abnormal only, and follows ISA-18.2 priority: P1 critical, P2 high, P3 low, plus a diagnostic category.
  • Analog values are shown as bars against operating zones, with the numeric value next to them. Trends sit next to values (sparklines).
  • Four-level display hierarchy: Level 1 overview, Level 2 unit control, Level 3 detail, Level 4 diagnostics and help.
  • Never rely on colour alone. Add shape, position or text for colour-blind operators.

ESD / Line Break Valve (LBV) logic

  • Detection: pressure rate-of-fall (dP/dt) over a short window plus low-low pressure, voted 2oo3 across transmitters. Inhibit during known transients (pump trip, valve operation, start-up).
  • Action: close the LBV on both sides of the break. Closure must not depend on comms. The RTU holds the trip latched until a deliberate reset.
  • Surge: upstream closure timing must limit surge below the MAOP margin. Verify stroke times during PST and SAT.
  • Bypass: time-limited, logged, and visible on every HMI level.

RTTM and mass-balance leak detection (LDS)

  • Mass balance: compensated inlet vs. outlet flow with line-pack correction. Simple, robust, slow for small leaks.
  • RTTM: a hydraulic model runs in parallel with measured data; residuals between model and measurements flag leaks and locate them. Needs good instrumentation and model tuning.
  • API RP 1130 (computational pipeline monitoring), API RP 1155 and API 1175 (leak detection programme) are the usual references. Specify sensitivity, reliability, robustness and accuracy as separate acceptance criteria and prove them in testing.
